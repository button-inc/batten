# Address versus inline transport

Generated by `mise run address-transport-bench`. A REPORT, never a gate: no task in `verify` or the hk gate reads it, and it exits 0 whatever the numbers say.

Sizes are CHARACTERS emitted, not tokens. A tokenizer is provider- and model-specific, and reporting characters is the honest unit available here — CLOUD-1367 §2 rules out a bytes-over-four approximation, and inventing one under a token label would be that approximation with a better name.

An address costs a fixed **69 characters** — the rendered grammar including `b3-`, the version and both separators, read from `identity::ADDRESS_RENDERED_LEN` rather than restated.

The declared threshold is `[capture] inline_max_bytes`, defaulting to **1024**. At or under it a payload renders inline; over it, addressed.

## What is NOT priced here, and why it is not an omission

- **Store and index writes, eviction misses and retries.** Those are costs in the store's own currency — bytes on disk and a filesystem round trip — and folding them into a character count would produce one number that is honest about neither. They belong beside `acquisition-bench`'s reading.
- **Privacy and safety caps.** They refuse to emit content at all, where this only changes the representation. One number serving both would let a measurement about cost decide what may leave the process.
- **A tokenizer.** See the header: characters are what can be measured without claiming a provider's tokenization.
